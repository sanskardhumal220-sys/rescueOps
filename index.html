Rescue Priority Scoring Methodology

RESCUE OPS uses a transparent, deterministic decision-support model designed for emergency coordinators. Because resources (search dogs, ATVs, extraction paramedics) are strictly limited during alpine storms, the algorithm quantifies multi-variable vulnerability into a clear, explainable priority score.

Operational Decision Tiers:

  • High Priority (≥ 70 pts): Immediate dispatch of nearest available rescue team or standby extraction unit.
  • Medium Priority (40–69 pts): Elevated surveillance, drone reconnaissance, or satellite relay check.
  • Low Priority (< 40 pts): Monitored check-ins; directed to hold position under shelter.
Decision-Support Notice: This scoring system is an automated decision-support recommendation tool designed for the Operation: Lost Signal scenario. It is intended to assist, not replace, certified incident commander judgment.
