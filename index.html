Rescue Priority Scoring Methodology

ResQ Command uses a weighted deterministic model designed for emergency coordinators. Because resources (air support, alpine rescue dogs, paramedic squads) are limited during storms, the algorithm quantifies vulnerability into a single transparent score.

Decision Breakdown:

  • High Priority (≥ 70 pts): Immediate dispatch of nearest search party or standby extraction unit.
  • Medium Priority (40–69 pts): En route check, drone flyover reconnaissance, or satellite relay alignment.
  • Low Priority (< 40 pts): Monitored check-ins; directed to hold position under natural shelter.
Official Notice: This scoring system is a simulated decision-support recommendation tool designed for the Operation: Lost Signal hackathon. It does not replace certified incident command verification.
